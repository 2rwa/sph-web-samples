SPH Web Samples

Small browser experiments for SPH implementations.

  • Salva 2D → Rust/WASM → Canvas — 216 particles rendered as simple Canvas dots.
  • Salva 2D WASM benchmark — adjustable from 100 to 10,000 particles.
  • Salva interactive obstacles — draw Circle, Box, Line, and Free Draw boundaries into a 1,000-particle SPH scene.
  • Salva 3D → WASM → Canvas dots — 512 three-dimensional SPH particles projected onto a 2D Canvas.

Official Salva examples2d web ports

  • Basic — three fluids, elasticity/viscosity, cosine basin.
  • Custom forces — two custom force fields, zero gravity.
  • Elasticity — two Young's moduli.
  • Layers — Salva interaction groups.
  • Surface tension — Akinci 2013 droplet.

Rapier × Salva two-way coupling
