Known behavior: occasional particles escape the box

This benchmark uses a hand-built SPH boundary made from particles, not a hard collision wall. The current setup uses two boundary layers, boundary spacing equal to one particle diameter, and a fixed SPH step of 1/120 s. A few particles can occasionally pass through the boundary.

The likely causes are boundary discretization and/or timestep size rather than a confirmed Salva bug. Useful follow-up checks:

  • denser or thicker boundary particles,
  • a smaller timestep such as 1/200 or 1/400 s,
  • comparison with a Rapier collider sampled as a Salva boundary.
