Custom forces → Salva/WASM → WebGPU surface + force volume

The 1,000-particle Salva custom-force sample becomes an implicit surface on a 56³ fluid density grid. The two upstream force sources at (+1,0,0) and (-1,0,0) are evaluated analytically per ray step and rendered as an analytic force-field volume around the fluid.
