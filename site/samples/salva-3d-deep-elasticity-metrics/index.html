Deep dive: Elasticity material response metrics

The two official elastic bodies have different Young's moduli. This page measures their response instead of only coloring them differently: Bounding-box stretch, COM, RMS speed, max speed, and an extent-volume proxy are tracked over time.

Bounding-box stretch is current XYZ extent divided by reset-time extent. It is a coarse deformation proxy, not a continuum strain tensor. RMS speed and max speed come directly from Salva particle velocities.
